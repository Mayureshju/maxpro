# Connected CRM acceptance

## GEM-13/14/15 — daily sales work
Given a legacy or new canonical lead, manual assignment/stage, table/board, inbox, analytics and operational filters read the same record. All leads, current demo member's leads (or explicit missing identity), unassigned, unanswered, due follow-ups, upcoming viewings and won/lost are accessible without re-entering data. Existing detailed filters remain. Mobile offers readable labelled records. Keyboard stage changes call existing stage command.

Given an active lead, scheduling requires a valid date/time, reason and available organization member; it creates only a local task. Completion requires an outcome. Reschedule/cancel/complete are revision checked and append activity. Due means an open recorded task with timestamp <= now; no suggestion becomes an actual reminder. Refresh preserves task history. Stale and foreign lead commands fail without mutation.

## GEM-21/02/03 — grounded assistance
Given explicitly labelled requirements in inbound messages, candidates cite their exact message/field evidence. Unlabelled or contradictory information is not silently confirmed. Review can correct candidates; explicit confirmation and current snapshot are required to persist requirements. Confirmed data remains editable. Matching uses only organization-scoped saved properties and confirmed fields, explains matching constraints and missing information, handles monthly/yearly rent vs sale total, and never infers availability or invents results.

Given a lead route, Copilot summarizes actual messages, linked property, requirements, source, last interaction and unanswered questions; suggested reply fills a draft only. Viewing preparation includes recorded status and no external booking claim. Agency briefing includes recorded unanswered/unassigned leads, due tasks, pending viewings, content blockers and campaign review work. Performance reports stage/source counts, documented outcomes and uncertainty, with links and no unsupported causal/revenue claims.

## GEM-01/16 — authority and compatibility
New fields round-trip through strict hydration, legacy absence remains valid, invalid extensions recover without deleting valid base leads. Central permissions and organization references guard all commands. Stale proposals and cross-organization matching fail closed. Public assistance receives no private state. Existing consent/STOP and campaign approval protections remain. No frontend credential, live sending, scheduler, provider connectivity or production security claim.

## GEM-17/19/22 — presentation
Public primary/secondary CTAs, existing screenshots, tours, disclosure, FAQ, contact preview, showcase and pricing remain functional. Lifecycle includes property, preparation, promotion, enquiry, follow-up/viewing and outcome/performance. Illustrative QAR 179/725/1,800+ and quotas remain pending approval. New interfaces support EN/AR, RTL, keyboard and six specified widths. Real rendered before/after captures and scripted counts are labelled evaluator evidence, not human research.
