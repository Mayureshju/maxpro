# Final prototype acceptance

## Organization demonstration — GEM-01/02/08/13/17/18/20/24
Given an existing Pearl Gate workspace, creating and selecting another fictional organization presents independent empty operations. Creating/editing records, preparing connectors, changing plan, ingesting leads and drafting campaigns affects only that organization. Switching back restores exact original IDs/revisions/data. Reload preserves selected organization and slices. Cross-organization IDs fail with recovery and cannot mutate records. Member-role, suspended-organization, quotas, source review and approval guards remain effective. Audit and subscription history show selected-organization events. Storage corruption is bounded and reported without fixture leakage.

## Complete journeys — GEM-02–21/24
All thirteen Phase 6 journey families are reachable and interactive with canonical records. Queue simulation never claims remote acceptance; proposals never claim model output; billing never claims purchase; domain previews never claim DNS verification. Empty, invalid, denied and stale revision states have useful recovery.

## Bilingual visual acceptance — GEM-03/22
Active pages use translated labels, validation, statuses and empty states; Arabic sets lang/dir and preserves LTR identifiers. At 1440/1024/768/375 widths controls remain reachable with no document overflow. Semantic labels, focus, keyboard navigation/dialog dismissal and accessible tables are checked. Actual running-app screenshots support visual review. Native-speaker and independent design approval are separately recorded.

## Non-UI acceptance — GEM-23
No backend service, vendor adapter, secret, real external mutation or deployment is added. Typed repository contracts specify organization context, revision, idempotency and discriminated errors; proposed Frappe mapping/permissions/jobs/audit are documented without pinning unverified framework/database versions. Full quality-gate results and unresolved prerequisites are recorded truthfully.
