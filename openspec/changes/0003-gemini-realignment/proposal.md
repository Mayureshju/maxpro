# 0003 — Gemini PRD realignment

## Why
The v2 prototype lacked the operating-system focus, connector provider detail, structured canonical editing, end-to-end simulated publishing, round-robin routing, white-label controls and Gemini-specific subscription model. Its generic portal names and pricing examples contradicted the user-provided Gemini PRD.

## Goal
Use Gemini as the primary scope and make the agency-first journey demonstrable in real source code without pretending third-party systems are integrated. Preserve the public property route group as a separate, unapproved backlog track.

## In scope
GEM-02–20, GEM-24: React demo interactions and navigation for property data, media, connector catalogue, feed/mapping previews, per-destination job ledger, lead routing, viewing, marketing/GA4/Google, sample entitlements, brand/domains, operations and analytics. Documents and testing for GEM-01, 03, 21–23.

## Out of scope
Production API, database, authentic user login, portal partner permissions, real AI, marketing spend, customer data, live billing or actual WhatsApp messages. Also no final approved pricing or branding.
