# Design

## Direction: the property as a thread
Keep forest/emerald, Manrope and IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Phase 15 foundation). Introduce a light editorial hero with an authentic property overview; four small lifecycle labels connect the subject to work. A forest feature-story section uses an explicit four-item controller at left and one focused real product image at right. No automatic rotation, fake app controls or nested frames. Every chapter names an outcome and links to the matching real demo route. Native image enlargement retains inspection without screenshot mode controls.

Homepage: business-value hero → four-chapter product story → source/proposal/human-review AI example → grouped marketing destinations → compact contextual CRM evidence → four audience situations → canonical sample pricing → final demo/contact invitation. Remove duplicate journey, benefit cards and homepage FAQ (FAQ remains a direct route).

Public family: shared page eyebrow, editorial title and contextual introduction; distinct purpose per page. Feature pages lead with cropped relevant evidence and decision/control context, supporting chapters and workflow links. Platform is a feature directory; agency page is a fictional day; how-it-works stays a detailed explicit tour; integrations remain a category/prerequisite directory; pricing retains readable matrix; FAQ/contact/about/resources keep task-appropriate content; entry uses matching typography and a concise journey instead of a decorative photo.

## Evidence and behavior
New native element captures in public/product/stories have their own SHA256/route/viewport/state provenance. Legacy overview assets are refreshed with their existing provenance contract. All screenshots are actual app captures, not generated UI. Public AI example invokes existing deterministic parser, maintains memory only, accepts/resets the example and never changes property records. Public assistant remains the sole product assistant with no private-store dependency.

## Interaction states
Four chapters: initial/create, selected/prepare/promote/follow; roving tab focus; horizontal arrows honor RTL, vertical arrows also supported, Home/End. Only active panel mounted. Mobile controls use wrapping two-column layout, content precedes visual, no essential sideways scroll. Images use native locale/mobile sources and enlargement. No autoplay. Focus rings and reduced motion retained. AI example: awaiting review/accepted/reset. Existing menu, FAQ, tour, directory, contact and entry loading/error states preserved.

## Research application
See docs/40-public-experience-redesign.md for primary references and rationale. Adopt principles of hierarchy, focused product evidence, progressive disclosure and outcome language; no copied layouts, text, branding or assets. Browser lab metrics are measurements, not field INP or invented Lighthouse scores.
